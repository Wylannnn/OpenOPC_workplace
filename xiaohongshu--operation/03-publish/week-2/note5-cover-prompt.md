Beneath this numeral, a thin horizontal accent line in plasma purple (#7B2FFF) extends from the glass panel's right edge to the canvas boundary. In the lower-right corner, three precision geometric circles in varying sizes are arranged in a diagonal cascade, each outlined in cyber cyan (#00D4FF) at 20% opacity, with one circle filled solid in plasma purple (#7B2FFF) at 15% opacity. The lighting is dramatic and directional — a single key light from the upper-left creates sharp highlights on the glass panel's edge while the rest of the scene recedes into deep shadow. - **Glass panel**: Frosted glass texture, backdrop blur, 2px cyan border, top-edge rim light

- **Circuit trace**: Thin continuous vector line weaving through circles
